# Product

VoltMender: Understand electrical equipment performance.

AI maintenance analytics software that connects electrical equipment readings and service histories to identify recurring performance problems for qualified technicians.

- Founder and CEO: Seyi Ogunbiyi
- Co-founder and CTO: Daniel Eze
- Industry: Electrical Engineering, Energy, Industrial Automation
- Customers: Electrical contractors, industrial facilities and equipment servicing companies
- Core features: Equipment anomaly detection, maintenance record analysis, recurring fault identification, inspection prioritization, maintenance performance reports
- MVP: Equipment reading imports and technician-reviewed alerts
- Planned AWS: SageMaker AI, IoT Core, Bedrock, S3, Lambda
- Business model: Subscriptions based on monitored assets
- Main action: Request a pilot
