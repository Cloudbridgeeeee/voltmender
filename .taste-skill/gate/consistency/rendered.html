How a VoltMender pilot runs

  1. Import equipment readings and service records

    You send meter and sensor exports plus twelve months of work orders. We match them to each asset.

  2. Review alerts with your technicians

    Alerts arrive each morning. Your technicians confirm or dismiss each one, and we tune the ranges.

  3. Read the maintenance performance report

    A monthly report per site: recurring faults, repeat repairs and inspection lists completed.

What maintenance managers ask before a pilot.

Ask about a pilot

What data do we need?

Equipment readings as CSV or meter exports, plus work orders with asset, date, fault and repair. Live sensor feeds are optional.

Does it control our equipment?

No. VoltMender reads data only and has no way to switch or change any equipment.

What does it cost?

A subscription priced by the number of monitored assets. We quote after the pilot.

Who reviews the alerts?

Your qualified technicians. Every alert is confirmed, changed or dismissed by a person.

Where is our data kept?

In an AWS account of its own, in the region you choose, encrypted at rest and in transit.
